<p class="mb-0"><i class="bi bi-info-circle me-2" aria-hidden="true"></i>This page uses simple words and short sentences. It is for everyone, also for people who find reading hard or do not speak Italian well. The page is in English. Some links open pages in Italian.</p>
</div>

<div class="facile-blocco">
<h2><span class="icona">📞</span> Emergency number</h2>
<p>For any emergency, call <strong>112</strong>.</p>

{{< pittogramma src="/pittogrammi/arasaac/112.png" alt="The number 112" size="large" >}}

<p>It is <strong>free</strong>. It works also with no phone credit.</p>
<a class="tel-grande" href="tel:112">📞 Call 112</a>
<p>When they answer, say:</p>
<ul class="facile-elenco">
<li>your name;</li>
<li>what happened;</li>
<li>where you are.</li>
</ul>
<p>Speak slowly. Do not end the call.</p>
</div>

<div class="facile-blocco">
<h2><span class="icona">🌍</span> If the ground shakes</h2>
<p>If you feel an earthquake:</p>

{{< pittogramma src="/pittogrammi/arasaac/nascondersi-tavolo.png" alt="A person hiding under a table during an earthquake" size="large" >}}

<ul class="facile-elenco">
<li>go under a table;</li>
<li>put your hands on your head;</li>
<li>stay still and wait.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Do not do these things:</strong></p>
<ul class="facile-elenco no">
<li>do not run away;</li>
<li>do not use the lift;</li>
<li>do not go to the window.</li>
</ul>
<p>When the shaking stops, go outside calmly if the place is not safe.</p>
</div>

<div class="facile-blocco">
<h2><span class="icona">🌧️</span> If it rains very hard</h2>
<p>If it rains a lot and the water rises:</p>

{{< pittogramma src="/pittogrammi/arasaac/alluvione.png" alt="A flood with high water" size="large" >}}

<ul class="facile-elenco">
<li>stay at home;</li>
<li>go to a higher floor;</li>
<li>turn off the power with an adult, only if you can do it safely.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Do not do these things:</strong></p>
<ul class="facile-elenco no">
<li>do not go to the cellar;</li>
<li>do not go through underpasses;</li>
<li>do not drink tap water if the authorities say it is not safe.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="facile-blocco">
<h2><span class="icona">🔥</span> If you see smoke</h2>
<p>If you see a lot of smoke at home:</p>

{{< pittogramma src="/pittogrammi/arasaac/incendio.png" alt="A fire" size="large" >}}

<ul class="facile-elenco">
<li>tell an adult at once;</li>
<li>go out of the house;</li>
<li>call <strong>112</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Do not do these things:</strong></p>
<ul class="facile-elenco no">
<li>do not go back inside;</li>
<li>do not take the lift;</li>
<li>do not open the windows.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="facile-blocco">
<h2><span class="icona">⛈️</span> If there is a storm</h2>
<p>If there is lightning and strong wind:</p>

{{< pittogramma src="/pittogrammi/arasaac/temporale.png" alt="A storm with lightning and rain" size="large" >}}

<ul class="facile-elenco">
<li>stay at home;</li>
<li>close the windows;</li>
<li>wait for it to pass.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Do not stay:</strong></p>
<ul class="facile-elenco no">
<li>under a tree;</li>
<li>in a swimming pool;</li>
<li>on the balcony.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="facile-blocco">
<h2><span class="icona">💨</span> If there is strong wind</h2>
<p>If the wind blows very hard:</p>

{{< pittogramma src="/pittogrammi/arasaac/vento.png" alt="Strong wind" size="large" >}}

<ul class="facile-elenco">
<li>stay at home;</li>
<li>close windows and doors;</li>
<li>stay away from the windows.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Do not do these things:</strong></p>
<ul class="facile-elenco no">
<li>do not stand under a tree;</li>
<li>do not walk near old walls;</li>
<li>do not go on the balcony.</li>
</ul>
<p>Things on balconies can fall. Bring them inside, if you can do it safely.</p>
</div>

<div class="facile-blocco">
<h2><span class="icona">💡</span> If the power goes out</h2>
<p>If there is no electricity at home:</p>

{{< pittogramma src="/pittogrammi/arasaac/torcia.png" alt="A torch that is on" size="large" >}}

<ul class="facile-elenco">
<li>stay calm;</li>
<li>use a torch with batteries;</li>
<li>keep the fridge closed;</li>
<li>wait for the power to come back.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Do not do these things:</strong></p>
<ul class="facile-elenco no">
<li>do not light candles alone;</li>
<li>do not take the lift;</li>
<li>do not use a barbecue or a brazier inside the house.</li>
</ul>
<p>If you use a machine that helps you breathe or live, call <strong>112</strong> at once.</p>
</div>

<div class="facile-blocco">
<h2><span class="icona">☀️</span> If it is very hot</h2>
<p>In summer there are very hot days:</p>

{{< pittogramma src="/pittogrammi/arasaac/caldo.png" alt="Very strong heat" size="large" >}}

<ul class="facile-elenco">
<li>drink water often;</li>
<li>stay at home in the hottest hours;</li>
<li>close the blinds when there is sun;</li>
<li>eat fruit and vegetables.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Do not do these things:</strong></p>
<ul class="facile-elenco no">
<li>do not go out in strong sun;</li>
<li>do not drink alcohol;</li>
<li>do not leave children or animals in the car.</li>
</ul>
<p>If you feel sick, ask an adult for help. If a person faints, call <strong>112</strong>.</p>
</div>

<div class="facile-blocco">
<h2><span class="icona">📱</span> If you get an IT-alert</h2>
<p><strong>IT-alert</strong> is a message the State sends to your phone when there is a big danger.</p>

<ul class="facile-elenco">
<li>the phone rings loud, also if it is silent;</li>
<li>a message appears on the screen;</li>
<li>read all the message calmly;</li>
<li>do what the message says.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Do not do these things:</strong></p>
<ul class="facile-elenco no">
<li>do not turn off the phone;</li>
<li>do not call 112 to say you got the message;</li>
<li>do not be afraid: it is there to protect you.</li>
</ul>
<p>The message reaches all the phones in an area. You are not alone.</p>
</div>

<div class="facile-blocco">
<h2><span class="icona">🔎</span> If you cannot find a family member</h2>
<p>In an emergency a family can get separated. Do not panic.</p>

<ul class="facile-elenco">
<li>wait at the family meeting point, if you chose one before;</li>
<li>if you have no meeting point, go home or to the nearest safe waiting area;</li>
<li>call a person who lives in another town, chosen by the family (a relative or a friend);</li>
<li>wait. The person will come to the same place.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Do not do these things:</strong></p>
<ul class="facile-elenco no">
<li>do not call 112 only to say you cannot find a relative, unless you think they are in serious danger;</li>
<li>do not go looking around: you may miss each other.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="facile-blocco">
<h2><span class="icona">🚪</span> If you must leave your home</h2>
<p>If Civil Protection or the Fire Brigade tell you to leave the house:</p>

<ul class="facile-elenco">
<li>take the emergency backpack;</li>
<li>take medicines, documents, phone and charger;</li>
<li>turn off water, power and gas if you have time;</li>
<li>close the door;</li>
<li>go to the safe waiting area they tell you.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Do not do these things:</strong></p>
<ul class="facile-elenco no">
<li>do not go back for forgotten things;</li>
<li>do not use the lift;</li>
<li>do not go into underpasses and do not walk under big trees.</li>
</ul>
<p>Help elderly neighbours or people who find it hard to walk, if you can do it safely.</p>
</div>

<div class="facile-blocco">
<h2><span class="icona">🐕</span> If you have an animal with you</h2>
<p>Dogs, cats and other animals must be protected too.</p>

<ul class="facile-elenco">
<li>keep the animal on a leash or in a carrier;</li>
<li>bring food, water and the animal's medicines;</li>
<li>bring the health booklet if you have it;</li>
<li>take it with you to the safe waiting area: animals are allowed.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Do not do these things:</strong></p>
<ul class="facile-elenco no">
<li>do not leave the animal alone at home;</li>
<li>do not leave the animal in the car in the heat;</li>
<li>do not set the animal free: it may get lost.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="facile-blocco">
<h2><span class="icona">🎒</span> Emergency backpack</h2>
<p>Keep a backpack at home with:</p>

{{< pittogramma src="/pittogrammi/arasaac/zaino.png" alt="An emergency backpack" size="large" >}}

<ul class="facile-elenco">
<li>water;</li>
<li>food that lasts a long time;</li>
<li>a torch with batteries;</li>
<li>a blanket;</li>
<li>a phone charger;</li>
<li>important medicines;</li>
<li>an identity document.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="facile-blocco">
<h2><span class="icona">📍</span> Where to go</h2>
<p>In Genzano di Roma there are <strong>safe places</strong>.</p>
<p>They are called <strong>waiting areas</strong> (in Italian, <em>aree di attesa</em>).</p>

{{< pittogramma src="/pittogrammi/iso7010/punto-raccolta.svg" alt="Assembly point sign" size="large" >}}

<p>They are open, wide spaces.</p>
<p>Go there if your home is not safe.</p>
<p><a href="/cartografia/" style="display: inline-block; background: #003366; color: #fff; padding: 0.8rem 1.2rem; border-radius: 8px; text-decoration: none; margin-top: 0.5rem;">See the waiting areas of Genzano di Roma (map)</a></p>
</div>

<div class="facile-blocco">
<h2><span class="icona">🆘</span> Who helps you</h2>
<p>In Italy there are people who help during emergencies.</p>

{{< pittogramma src="/pittogrammi/arasaac/volontario.png" alt="A civil protection volunteer" size="large" >}}

<ul class="facile-elenco">
<li><strong>Fire Brigade</strong>: they put out fires and make places safe.</li>
<li><strong>Carabinieri and Police</strong>: they help when there is danger.</li>
<li><strong>Medical rescue</strong>: they care for injured people.</li>
<li><strong>Civil Protection volunteers</strong>: they help people.</li>
</ul>
<p>To ask for help in an emergency, call <strong>112</strong>.</p>
</div>
