This is the English text of «Il libro che si esplora», the civil protection book of the municipal volunteer group of Genzano di Roma. Each chapter describes a situation and asks what you would do. Choose your answer, then open **Show the answer** to check it.

The [interactive 3D book](/formazione/libro-che-si-esplora/) is in Italian. The pages linked in each chapter are in Italian too.

**In an emergency, call 112**, the single European emergency number. It is free and works from any phone.
