In a disaster — an earthquake, a flood, a fire, an evacuation or a long interruption of essential services — some people may have particular needs and require clear, simple and immediate tools.

The Municipal Volunteer Group of Civil Protection of Genzano di Roma has gathered on this page a set of **printable A4 kits**, designed to help citizens, families, care workers, volunteers, teachers and care facilities cope better with the first difficult hours.

> **This is an English overview of the Disaster Kits.** The detailed kits and the printable A4 cards (for newborns, older people, disability, pets and the other situations below) are currently **available in Italian only**. For the full materials, see the [Italian version of this page](/formazione/kit-calamita/).

The materials are **practical support tools**: checklists, communication cards, routines, maps, reminders, basic guidance and simple activities. They draw on standards, good practice and Italian and international institutional references, but they **do not replace** the medical, psychological, veterinary, care or operational instructions given by the competent authorities and qualified professionals.

> **Choose the situation below that is closest to your needs**: you will reach the most suitable cards. If in doubt, first read the guide for care workers or the introductory page of the relevant kit.

The cards are designed to be printed in **black and white** as well, with simple graphics, short texts and a format suited to on-the-spot use.

## Children and newborns

### I am a parent, educator or volunteer with children aged 3–14
Cards for active distraction, routines, communication, orientation, inclusion and light emotional support. They are grouped by age range and include activities for young children, older children and special communication needs.

→ **[Disaster Kit for Children](/formazione/kit-calamita-bambini/)**

### I am the parent of a newborn aged 0–3
Cards on keeping the baby warm, feeding, hygiene, changing, breastfeeding, safe formula feeding, and warning signs to report promptly to health staff or by calling 112.

→ **[Disaster Kit for Newborns aged 0–3](/formazione/kit-calamita-neonati/)**

## Older people and carers

### I am an older person, or I live with an older person
Cards on orientation, medicines, contacts, essential items, routines, communication needs, home safety and first useful information in case of evacuation or of staying at home.

→ **[Disaster Kit for Older People at Home](/formazione/kit-calamita-anziani/)**

### I am a family carer for a dependent person
Cards for people who look after a bedridden family member, or a relative with dementia, severe disability, chronic conditions or complex needs. They help you organise information, therapies, contacts, aids and care breaks.

→ **[Disaster Kit for Family Carers](/formazione/kit-calamita-caregiver-familiari/)**

## Health and care facilities

### I am a director, worker or contact person in a care home, long-term care unit, hospice or care facility
Cards on preparedness, continuity of care, transfers, communication with families, and support for vulnerable residents, with attention to comfort, orientation and sensory needs.

→ **[Disaster Kit for Care Facilities](/formazione/kit-calamita-strutture-sanitarie/)**

## Disability, health and therapies

### I am an adult with a disability living in the community
Cards for physical, sensory, cognitive, communication or psychological disability: a personal information card, an evacuation checklist, contacts, aids and essential needs.

→ **[Disaster Kit for Adults with Disability](/formazione/kit-calamita-disabilita-adulti/)**

### I depend on an ongoing life-saving therapy
Cards for dialysis, oxygen therapy, essential medicines, transplants, cancer therapies, rare diseases or electro-medical devices. The aim is to support continuity of treatment, documents and medical contacts.

→ **[Disaster Kit for Life-Saving Therapies](/formazione/kit-calamita-terapie-salvavita/)**

## Pregnancy, birth and new mothers

### I am pregnant or I have just given birth
Cards to prepare essential health information, warning signs, a personal kit, contacts, medicines, the newborn's needs and information to give to rescuers or health staff.

→ **[Disaster Kit for Pregnancy and New Mothers](/formazione/kit-calamita-gravidanza/)**

## Social and language exclusion

### I am, or I work with, people who are homeless
Cards for basic identification, contacts, essential needs, guidance to services, medicines, accompanying animals and low-threshold support.

→ **[Disaster Kit for Homeless People](/formazione/kit-calamita-senza-fissa-dimora/)**

### I am a foreign national, or I support people learning Italian as a second language
Cards for essential communication, personal information, basic needs, an emergency phrasebook and multilingual support. Translations should be checked with mediators or native speakers before being used in real situations.

→ **[Disaster Kit for Italian as a Second Language](/formazione/kit-calamita-italiano-l2/)**

## Pets

### I have a pet, or I support families with animals
Cards for dogs, cats, exotic animals and animals taken along during an evacuation: documents, carrier, medicines, food, stress management, identification and veterinary contacts.

→ **[Disaster Kit for Pets](/formazione/kit-calamita-animali/)**

## Civil Protection volunteers

### I am a volunteer and I want to take care of myself during and after a shift
Cards on self-care, decompression, end-of-shift routines, signs of fatigue, stress management, sleep, nutrition and peer support.

→ **[Disaster Kit for Civil Protection Volunteers](/formazione/kit-calamita-volontari-pc/)**

## References and good practice {#standard-adottati}

The kits draw on institutional, humanitarian, health and inclusion references. This list is intended as guidance for care workers, teachers, volunteers and public bodies who wish to reuse or adapt the materials.

In case of doubt, the instructions of the competent authorities, the local procedures and the guidance of the professionals in charge always take precedence.

Useful references: the Agency for Digital Italy (AgID) and Designers Italia, the Italian Civil Protection Department, the Regions, the Municipalities, the local health authorities, the competent authorities, the Sphere Standards, WHO, IFRC, UNHCR, IOM, the IFE Core Group, the IAWG, the NCTSN, HelpAge International, the WSAVA, the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities, ARASAAC, augmentative and alternative communication, and ISO 7010.

## How to use the kits

1. Identify the situation of the person or of the family.
2. Open the corresponding kit.
3. First read the introductory page or the guide for care workers, if there is one.
4. Print only the cards you really need.
5. Present the cards calmly, without turning them into a task or a test.
6. Fill in the essential information legibly.
7. Update medicines, contacts, therapies, useful numbers and documents from time to time.
8. For any medical, psychological, veterinary or care problem, always contact professionals and the competent authorities.

## Reuse and attribution

The kit materials are meant to be useful to the community and may be reused, adapted and printed by municipalities, Civil Protection groups, schools, associations, care facilities and care workers.

When the materials are copied, adapted or redistributed, a citation of the source is appreciated:

> "Adapted from the Disaster Kits of the Municipal Volunteer Group of Civil Protection of Genzano di Roma — www.protezionecivilegenzano.it".

Any third-party images or resources keep their original licence.

The kits may be improved over time. To report needs, suggest new cards or point out inaccuracies, write to [segreteria@protezionecivilegenzano.it](mailto:segreteria@protezionecivilegenzano.it).
